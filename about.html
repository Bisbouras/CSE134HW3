About me

Who I am

I'm Christos-Iason Enotiadis, Chris for short, and Bisbo online. I grew up in Athens, Greece, went to a British school there, and moved to San Diego in 2022 to study computer science at UCSD.

How I got into games

I started making games as a teenager in Unreal Engine 3. From there I moved to GameMaker Studio and then to Unreal Engine 5, which is what I used professionally at Couch Heroes.

These days I care as much about why a mechanic works as how it is built. KILLSTREAK started as a design question, not a tech demo: what happens to a shooter when neither player can see the whole picture?

How I think about design

I start with the fundamental concept of information. No matter what I am designing, I have to think about all the information that I want to be accessible to the user, and what must not be.

Outside of making games

I write and produce music and play guitar, write poetry, and paraglide when I get the chance. I also play a lot of games, competitively in rhythm games and for far too many hours in League of Legends, which is a useful education in what makes a competitive game feel fair.
